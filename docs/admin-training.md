# Admin dashboard – staff guide

Sign in with your work email. What you see depends on your role:
**Staff** – daily work. **Manager** – also Reports, Settings (view) and Audit log. **Owner** – everything, including staff accounts and settings.

## Daily routine (10 minutes)

1. **Overview** → "Needs attention": booking requests, scooters awaiting handover, KYC to review, open service requests.
2. **Overdue and due soon** table on Overview: call riders who are late.
3. **Service requests**: assign anything new.

## New rider

1. Rider signs up in the app and uploads Aadhaar, PAN and driving licence.
2. **Customers** → filter *KYC: Needs review* → open the rider → **Review all**.
   - In the viewer, check name and number match the profile and the photo is readable. Use zoom/rotate.
   - **Approve** or **Reject** each document (rejection needs a reason the rider will see).
3. When the three required documents are approved, click **Approve KYC**. The rider gets a notification.

## Giving out a scooter

1. **Booking requests** → **Allocate** → choose an available scooter (the rider's preferred one is pre-selected).
2. The rider signs the agreement in the app. The rental shows *Awaiting handover*.
3. When the rider arrives: **Rentals** → open the rental → **Start handover**.
   - Fill the checklist, take photos, tick deposit + first week if you collected them and choose cash / UPI / bank.
   - Tick "Customer checked this with me" → **Complete handover**. The next due date is set to 7 days from today.

## Payments

- UPI payments in the app are confirmed automatically. You don't need to do anything.
- Cash / offline UPI / bank: open the rental → **Record payment**. Choose *Rent* to move the due date by 7 days.
  Put the UPI reference in Notes. Every offline payment is logged with your name.
- **Payments** page: filter by date, status, method. Download or regenerate a receipt from the row.

## Returns

Open the rental → **Record return**. The outstanding balance is shown at the top.
Record any payment first, or write how it was settled (e.g. "Adjusted ₹1,820 from deposit").
Choose whether the scooter is available again or goes to maintenance.

## Holding renewal

If a scooter needs inspection or there is a dispute: rental → **Hold renewal** with a reason. The rider can't pay
online until you click **Allow renewal**.

## Scooters

**Scooters** → **Add scooter** or click a row to edit. Scooter IDs must be unique. Reserved/rented scooters change
status only through their rental. Retire scooters instead of deleting them once they have rental history.

## Reports (manager / owner)

Collections (daily/weekly/monthly), customer-wise, outstanding & overdue, scooter utilisation.
Choose a date range → **Excel** or **PDF**.

## Settings (owner)

Rent, deposit, late fee, grace days, support numbers, company details, pickup locations, agreement text,
staff accounts. Changing rent affects **new** rentals only, unless you use *apply it to running rentals*.
**Agreement wording must be reviewed by a lawyer** before go-live.
